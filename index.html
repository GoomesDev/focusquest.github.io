Focus Quest

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

Focus Quest is developed and published by Henrique Gomes Dev.

This Privacy Policy explains how Focus Quest handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Focus Quest does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developer or external servers.

The app does not require an account, login, registration, name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone access, or advertising identifier.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Focus Quest stores certain information locally on your device in order to provide its core functionality. This may include:

  • total XP and level progression;
  • completed focus sessions;
  • accumulated focus time;
  • current and longest streak;
  • quest completion counts;
  • mission reward status;
  • language preference;
  • onboarding status;
  • sound and vibration preferences;
  • active quest and timer timestamps;
  • local notification identifiers;
  • other settings required for the app to operate.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to servers operated by Henrique Gomes Dev.

3. Notifications

Focus Quest may use local device notifications to notify you about timers, pauses, resumed sessions, and completed focus quests. These notifications are scheduled and processed locally on your device.

Focus Quest does not use remote push notifications and does not request or transmit a push notification token to the developer.

Notification content may appear on your device's lock screen depending on your operating system settings.

4. Device Permissions

Focus Quest may request or use permissions required for its functionality, including notifications, vibration, and alarms or exact alarms used for focus timers.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, Bluetooth devices, or advertising identifier.

5. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The current version of Focus Quest does not include advertising SDKs, behavioral advertising, analytics services, user tracking, or third-party crash-reporting services.

Focus Quest does not sell personal data.

6. Accounts and Authentication

Focus Quest does not require users to create an account and does not provide login or registration functionality.

7. Third-Party Technologies

Focus Quest is built using technologies including React Native and Expo. The application also uses local storage and notification libraries to provide its functionality.

Based on the current implementation, Focus Quest does not intentionally transmit application usage data or personal information to the developer's servers.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Focus Quest data is stored locally on your device for as long as the application remains installed or until the data is removed.

The app's reset functionality can remove gameplay and focus progress. Some preferences, such as language or application settings, may remain after an in-app reset.

You can remove all locally stored Focus Quest data by clearing the application's storage/data through Android settings or by uninstalling Focus Quest from your device.

Because Focus Quest does not maintain user accounts or remote user data, there is no remote account or server-side data to delete.

9. Children's Privacy

Focus Quest does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The current version does not include age collection, date-of-birth collection, parental-control functionality, or child-specific profiles.

10. Security

Because application progress and preferences are stored locally, their security is primarily provided by the security mechanisms of your device and Android operating system.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Focus Quest's functionality changes, including if future versions introduce features such as advertising, analytics, cloud synchronization, accounts, or other third-party services.

Any updated version will include a revised effective date.
